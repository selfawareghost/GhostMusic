Ghost plays full songs from your Spotify Premium account. Spotify asks every personal app for its own ID, so there's a one-time setup of about 10 minutes.

  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify Premium account. Accept the terms if asked.
  2. Click Create app. Name: Ghost. Description: My music site.
  3. In Redirect URIs, paste this exact address and click Add:
  4. Under "Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?" tick Web API and Web Playback SDK. Agree and click Save.
  5. Open the app's Settings, copy the Client ID, paste it below and press Connect Spotify.
